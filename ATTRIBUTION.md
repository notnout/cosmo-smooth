## Cosmo's Cosmic Adventure (1992)

*Cosmo's Cosmic Adventure* was released for MS-DOS by Apogee Software, Ltd. in
1992. The original game artwork, levels, music, and sound effects are
© 1992 Apogee Software, Ltd. and are **not** included in this repository.

- Todd J. Replogle — programming and level design
- Stephen A. Hornback — graphics
- Scott Miller — production
- Allen H. Blum III — level editor
- Bobby Prince — AdLib music
- id Software — audio/music routines

## Open-Source Upstream Projects

`cosmo-smooth` builds directly on these open-source projects:

- **[Cosmore](https://github.com/smitelli/cosmore)** — source code
  reconstruction of *Cosmo's Cosmic Adventure* v1.20.
  © 2020–2024 Scott Smitelli and contributors (MIT License).
- **[Cosmodoc](https://cosmodoc.org/)** — technical teardown of the original
  engine and data formats by Scott Smitelli.
- **[digows/cosmo](https://github.com/digows/cosmo)** — portable C11 EGA
  emulation layer and Cosmore patches.
  © 2026 Rodrigo P. Fraga (MIT License).
- **[Cosmo Enhanced (`GITitdoneboi/enhanced-cosmo`)](https://github.com/GITitdoneboi/enhanced-cosmo)** —
  Smooth Gameplay sub-tile camera, sprite, star, and effect interpolation
  patches and compositor algorithm (MIT License).
- **[Open Watcom v2](https://github.com/open-watcom/open-watcom-v2)** &
  **CauseWay DOS Extender** — 32-bit DOS compiler and public-domain protected-mode
  extender stubbed into the published executables.
